Interpretation route used throughout:

Core classroom statement:
    If P_k = x, then approximately k% of the ordered observations are at or
    below x, and approximately (100-k)% are at or above x.

Important qualification:
- Percentiles describe relative position in an ordered distribution.
- A percentile number is not a percent of the raw value.
- When context changes, "higher percentile" does not automatically mean
  "better" performance (for example, race time where lower is better).
- Comparing percentile ranks across different groups/tests describes relative
  standing, not necessarily a larger raw score.
